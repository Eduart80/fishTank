# Creature Roster (official)

38 creatures across 5 worlds. Ends with the Kraken.
All are real animals or public-domain myths (no movie/game IP).

Status: ✅ in game · ⬜ planned
Build rule: one world at a time, next world only after playtests show players reach the current world's boss.

## Art note

Worlds 1–2 can use procedural shapes. Worlds 3–5 (sharks, whales, myths) need real 3D models
(licensed Toolbox models or a commission — main use of the art budget).
