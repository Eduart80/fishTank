# Market research: what Roblox players spend time and money on (2026-11-13)

Third-party trackers and blogs; revenue numbers are ESTIMATES (Roblox doesn't publish them) and sources disagree.

## Time
- Simulation is the biggest genre: ~36% of active players; then Survival (growing), Roleplay. [Statroblox](https://statroblox.com/guides/most-popular-roblox-genres)
- All-time visits: Brookhaven RP (~85B), Blox Fruits, Adopt Me!. [Cubix](https://www.cubix.co/blog/top-10-most-popular-roblox-games/)
- Live, Nov 12 2026: Steal An Egg ~848k CCU, Brookhaven, Throw Food For Animals, Blox Fruits. [Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)

## Money
- Simulation is also the top-earning genre (est.). [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/genres)
- Top earners: Rivals (skins + battle pass), Blox Fruits, Brookhaven, Steal An Egg, Murder Mystery 2, Anime Adventures. [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform), [BloxRant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-top-earning-games)
- Players pay for eggs, pets, limited items, crates, boosts, skins: small REPEAT purchases.
- Earnings/day = payer % x spend per payer. [Creator Hub](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)
- Realistic payer share ~1.8% (case study); 5-8% "excellent" (unsourced). [RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/case-studies/tycoon-monetization-overhaul)
- Pass-only games plateau; developer products keep earning. [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/developer-products-vs-game-passes-you-re-using-both-wrong)
- Purchases reportedly fall off above ~R$400-600 (unverified). Low sales = too pricey, shop hard to find, items unappealing (Creator Hub).

## Closest model: Grow a Garden (21M+ CCU)
Same loop as ours (buy seed -> grows, also offline -> sell -> better seeds), plus:
- Weather mutations: rain/thunder give a chance of a more valuable mutated crop. [Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/weather-events-grow-a-garden)
- Rare mutations from pets + weather. [booboo](https://booboo.cc/blog/grow-a-garden-guide-how-to-play-mutations-pets)
- Timed seed-shop restocks (rare seeds only sometimes in stock).
- Pets hatched from eggs, 3 equipped, boost the garden.
- Weekend admin events (~15M CCU spikes). [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-breaks-all-time-player-record)
- Steal a Brainrot: spread via TikTok/YouTube memes; ~1/3 of Grow a Garden's playtime. Shareability gets players in, depth keeps them.

## What we take from it (ranked)
1. Weather mutations: storm -> Stormborn, night -> Moonlit variants that sell for more (built 2026-11-13).
2. Rare Egg Roll (Robux developer product, repeatable).
3. Limited rare-egg restocks.
4. Big weekend event ("Fish Frenzy").
5. Clip-worthy moments for TikTok (Kraken release, market crash).
